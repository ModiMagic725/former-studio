Shipping policy

  • Free standard shipping on bags above ₹3,999, otherwise ₹149.
  • Express shipping is ₹399.
  • Promo code FREESHIP removes the standard shipping fee.

Processing time

In this sample policy, orders are packed within 1 to 2 working days. Delivery estimates start after packing and are demo assumptions of 3 to 6 working days (standard) or 1 to 2 (express).

Delivery area

The demo assumes delivery within India. PIN-code checks on product pages validate the format only and show a sample estimate.

Return eligibility

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and have tags attached.
  • Return within 7 days of delivery.
  • Limited-edition and final-sale pieces are exchange-only.

How a return works

  1. Request

    Contact customer care with your order reference and the item to return.

  2. Pack

    Pack the item unworn with tags in its original packaging.

  3. Send

    Hand the parcel to the courier using the details we send you.

  4. Refund or exchange

    Once checked, we refund to the original payment method or ship your exchange.

Exchange and refund

Exchanges for a different size ship once the original arrives, subject to stock. Refunds are issued within 5 to 7 working days of the return being checked in this sample policy.
