Origin / 2019

One tee,
one argument

FORME began as a single T-shirt and a disagreement: that a garment should say something quiet and last a long time. The first run was forty pieces, cut and sewn by a small team around one long table.

Studio 09 is still that room, only with more rails. We design in small batches, test every pattern on real bodies, and write down exactly what each garment is made of.

This story is fictional sample content for a portfolio project.

Philosophy

01

Fewer, better

A smaller range lets us spend real time on the cut, the cloth and the finishing of each piece.

02

Say what it is

Fabric, fit, care and model measurements on every product page. If we do not know something, we leave it out.

03

Wear it your way

Cuts are designed to be styled loosely, tucked, layered or left alone. No single correct look.

Materials and construction

Built to
be worn

Cotton
Combed and heavyweight jersey, 240 to 300 gsm, garment washed.
Wool
Dense wool blends for coats and knitwear.
Denim
13 oz selvedge, unwashed, with a locking button fly.
Hardware
Brushed steel, nickel-free plating and taped seams on technical pieces.

Creative
process

01

Sketch

Every piece starts as a drawing and a sentence about what it is for.

02

Pattern

Paper patterns are cut and adjusted on real bodies, not only on forms.

03

Sample

We wear each sample for weeks and wash it often before approving it.

04

Small batch

Production runs stay small, so there is little left over and little to discount.
