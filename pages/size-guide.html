Fit definitions

    Between sizes?

    • For regular fits, take the larger size if you like room and the smaller if you like a close line.
    • For oversized pieces, size down if you want less drop at the shoulder.
    • For raw denim, buy true to size. It shrinks about 3% on the first wash.
    • Still unsure? Check the model measurements on the product page, or ask customer care.
