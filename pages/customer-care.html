Orders and delivery

Standard delivery is estimated at 3 to 6 working days and express at 1 to 2 working days. These are demo assumptions, not a courier promise.

  • Free standard shipping above ₹3,999, otherwise ₹149.
  • Express delivery is ₹399.
  • This demo does not place real orders or connect to any courier.

Returns and exchanges

Unworn, unwashed items with tags can be returned within 7 days of delivery in this sample policy. Limited-edition pieces are exchange-only.

Payment

General demo content: the checkout shows UPI, card and cash on delivery as options only. No payment is processed and no card, UPI or bank details are ever collected on this site.

Product care

  • Cotton: machine wash cold, inside out, line dry.
  • Wool and mohair: dry clean or hand wash cold, dry flat.
  • Raw denim: wash rarely, cold, inside out.
  • Technical shells: wipe clean or gentle cold wash. Do not tumble dry.
